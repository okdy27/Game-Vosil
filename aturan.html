Vosil Survival

Ringkasnya: game ini gratis. Hadiah VOS bersifat bonus dari komunitas, bukan penghasilan, bukan investasi, dan tidak dijamin. Versi ini masih BETA, jadi aturan dan angka bisa berubah.

1. Gratis dimainkan

Kamu tidak perlu membayar apa pun untuk bermain. Game tidak pernah meminta frasa pemulihan (seed phrase) atau kunci pribadi dompet. Jangan pernah membagikannya kepada siapa pun, termasuk yang mengaku admin.

2. Poin dan VOS

  • Poin didapat dari bermain. Batas poin per dompet per hari bersifat dinamis (antara 200 dan 1.000 poin) dan menyesuaikan jumlah pemain aktif, supaya anggaran adil bagi semua.
  • 1.000 poin = 1 VOS. Penukaran minimal 5.000 poin dan maksimal 5.000 poin per dompet per hari.
  • Penukaran ditinjau dahulu (sekitar 24–48 jam) dan dibayar sesuai kuota harian global. Jika kuota hari itu habis, permintaan masuk antrean.
  • VOS dikirim di jaringan World Chain ke dompet yang kamu hubungkan. Kirim ke alamat yang salah tidak bisa dibatalkan.
  • Anggaran hadiah terbatas dan dapat disesuaikan atau dihentikan sewaktu-waktu. Kami tidak menjamin jumlah atau waktu hadiah.

3. Fair play

  • Satu orang, satu dompet. Dilarang memakai bot, skrip, banyak akun, atau memanipulasi game (termasuk mengubah data permainan).
  • Pelanggaran dapat membuat poin dibatalkan, penukaran ditolak, atau dompet diblokir tanpa pemberitahuan.
  • Nama pemain di arena tampil kepada pemain lain. Dilarang memakai nama kasar, pelecehan, atau meniru admin. Nama yang melanggar diganti otomatis.

4. Arena online (uji coba)

Arena masih tahap uji dan belum memberi poin atau hadiah. Arena bisa penuh atau berhenti sewaktu-waktu karena batas layanan; mode solo tetap tersedia.

5. Data yang disimpan

  • Alamat dompet, jumlah poin, catatan waktu bermain per hari, riwayat penukaran.
  • Penanda jaringan dan perangkat dalam bentuk sandi acak (hash) untuk mencegah akun ganda. Kami tidak menyimpan nama asli, e-mail, atau nomor telepon.
  • Nama pemain yang kamu ketik tersimpan di perangkatmu dan dikirim ke arena saat kamu bergabung.

6. Penafian

Game disediakan "apa adanya" tanpa jaminan. Nilai token dapat berubah dan ini bukan nasihat keuangan. Kamu bertanggung jawab atas dompet dan keputusanmu sendiri. Game dimaksudkan untuk pemain berusia 13 tahun ke atas.

7. Pertanyaan & laporan

Hubungi pengelola lewat komunitas Telegram Vosil. Jangan pernah mengirim seed phrase atau kunci pribadi.

In short: the game is free. VOS rewards are a community bonus, not income, not an investment, and not guaranteed. This is a BETA, so rules and numbers may change.

1. Free to play

You never need to pay to play. The game never asks for your seed phrase or private key. Never share them with anyone, including people claiming to be admins.

2. Points and VOS

  • Points are earned by playing. The daily points limit per wallet is dynamic (between 200 and 1,000 points) and adjusts to the number of active players so the budget stays fair.
  • 1,000 points = 1 VOS. Minimum exchange is 5,000 points and the maximum is 5,000 points per wallet per day.
  • Exchanges are reviewed first (about 24–48 hours) and paid within the global daily quota. If the day's quota is used up, requests wait in a queue.
  • VOS is sent on World Chain to the wallet you connected. Sending to a wrong address cannot be undone.
  • The reward budget is limited and may be adjusted or stopped at any time. Amounts and timing are not guaranteed.

3. Fair play

  • One person, one wallet. Bots, scripts, multiple accounts, and tampering with the game are prohibited.
  • Violations may lead to cancelled points, rejected exchanges, or a blocked wallet without notice.
  • Player names in the arena are visible to others. Offensive names, harassment, or impersonating admins are not allowed and are replaced automatically.

4. Online arena (test)

The arena is in testing and gives no points or rewards yet. It may be full or stop at any time due to service limits; solo mode remains available.

5. Data we store

  • Wallet address, points, daily play-time counters, exchange history.
  • Network and device markers as random hashes to prevent duplicate accounts. We do not store your real name, e-mail, or phone number.
  • The player name you type is stored on your device and sent to the arena when you join.

6. Disclaimer

The game is provided "as is" without warranty. Token value can change and this is not financial advice. You are responsible for your wallet and decisions. The game is intended for players aged 13 and over.

7. Questions & reports

Contact the team through the Vosil Telegram community. Never send a seed phrase or private key.
